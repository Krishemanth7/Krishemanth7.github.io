My Power BI journey 📊

From being curious about dashboards to learning how to build them.

Well, everything was going pretty smoothly with my AP processing until 2024, when I got promoted to Process Lead. That's when I got an opportunity to work with the PMO team. It was also the first time I got to see how Power BI actually works behind the dashboards.

Before that, I had seen many BI dashboards as an end user. I was always curious about them — they looked attractive, yet managed to present so much information in a simple and understandable way. I always wanted to know what actually goes on behind those dashboards and how they are created.

So I asked my lead if I could learn Power BI, and thankfully, he generously gave me the opportunity. That's where my Power BI journey began.

I started by creating reports using our day-to-day AP records from an end-user perspective. As I became more comfortable, I started exploring filters, slicers, different visualizations and data schemas, and gradually moved towards creating interactive dashboards.

What started as simple curiosity slowly turned into something I genuinely enjoyed. I began looking at our AP data differently — not just as records to be processed, but as information that could actually tell a story.
